# 🔥 STREET BACHATA BATTLE 🔥

The ultimate clash of **musicality, attitude, and creativity**.

The best dancers will go head-to-head in a battle where **technique alone won’t be enough**.  
Here, you’ll need **style**, **character**, **interpretation**… and above all a **real identity**.

## ⚡ Explosive prelims

Each dancer has **45 seconds** to convince the judges and secure a spot in the **final bracket**.

## ⚔️ Final bracket

The **8 selected dancers** face the invited **guests** head-to-head in intense duels:  
**Round of 8 → Quarter-finals → Semi-finals → Final**

## ⏱️ Battle format

- Up to semi-finals: **1 minute** per dancer  
- Final: **2 rounds** per dancer to fight for the title.

## 💰 On the line

**€1,000** cash prize for the winner.

## 🎭 Fusion allowed — but watch out

**Bachata** must stay at the **heart** of every round.  
Dancers are judged on how far they can push the limits while keeping the **essence of bachata**.

## ⚠️ Pure improv

**No pre-made choreography — 100% improvisation.**

Tracks are **random** and will **never** be shared in advance.  
Each dancer discovers their music **the moment they step on floor**.

---

🔥 **More than a battle.** A stage. An identity. A war of energy.

**Who will move the crowd… and walk away with the title?**

# 🔥 RULES – STREET BACHATA BATTLE 🔥

## 🟥 1. Prelims in front of the judges

Each dancer performs **individually** for the judges.

- **Set length:** 45 seconds per dancer.  
- After all rounds, **8 dancers** are selected.  
- They join the **final bracket** alongside guests who are already qualified.

## 🟥 2. Final bracket

From the round of 8, each selected dancer **faces a guest**.

**Progression:** Round of 8 → Quarter-finals → Semi-finals → Final.

**Set lengths:**

- Up to semi-final: **one 1-minute round** per dancer  
- Final: **two 1-minute rounds** per dancer

## 💰 3. Prize

The winner takes home **€1,000**.

## ⚠️ 4. Style & artistic rules

**Fusion** is allowed (mixing styles).  
However, **bachata must remain dominant**.

If the judges feel bachata is **not present enough**,  
👉 **the round may be penalized.**
