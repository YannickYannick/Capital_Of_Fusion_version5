## 🌴 Paris Bachata Vibe Festival — The full experience

This festival is not a string of parties.  
It is an emotional arc.

A build-up.  
A peak.  
A come-down.  
A release.

👉 Total immersion where music, dress code, staging and energy become one.

---

## 🔮 Thursday — Urban Vibe (Purple)

**Dress code:** streetwear, lifestyle, urban  
**Vibe:** underground / city energy

This is your entry into the festival.  
A raw, modern world, connected to the street.

**Musically:**

Mostly today's European bachata sounds.

Plus an opening to fusion with influences from big cities: hip-hop, reggaeton, amapiano…

- Remixes allowed (but in the minority)
- An experimental, current vibe

---

## 🌵 Friday — Añejo Vibe (Green & Orange)

**Dress code:** Coachella vibe, sunglasses  
**Vibe:** warmth, nostalgia, groove

**Musically:**

- ❌ No remixes
- ✅ 50% moderna bachata classics
- ✅ 50% current hits

🔥 **+ Live bachata concert (full band)**

## 🌊 Saturday — Capital of Fusion + XXL Pool Party (Yellow & Blue)

**Dress code:** beach / swimwear  
**Vibe:** explosion, freedom, controlled chaos

This is the festival's climax.

**Musically:**

- Full fusion

## 🌌 Sunday — Smooth Vibe (final blackout)

**Dress code:** free / soft / elegant  
**Vibe:** introspection, connection, letting go

**Musically:**

- Remixes allowed
- BPM gradually comes down
